MotionScout

Motion’s official creator ranks, social stats where available, and a deck builder for any star cap.

How to play

Seven steps from your first pack to Friday’s results. Rules from docs.motion.game.

01/07Packs, Marketplace, trade-ins

Get cards

  • Starter Pack: $100 for 7 random cards, each a different creator.
  • Rarity odds per card: Common 75%, Rare 20%, Epic 4.5%, Legendary 0.5%.
  • High-star creators show up less often in packs.
  • Marketplace: buy a specific card or place a bid. Sellers pay a 3% fee.
  • Trade in 12 cards you don’t need for a new Starter Pack. Traded cards are burned for good.
02/07Stars, rarity, the creator

Read a card

  • Stars (1–8): the creator’s form over the last 14 days compared with everyone else. They count toward the star cap and set pack odds, not points.
  • Rarity: how scarce the card is. It sets the Score Boost.
  • Top 20 / Rank / Socials: top-20 finishes in official tournaments, rank on the Motion Leaderboard (Pack Shop), and how many of the 4 supported platforms they’re active on.
  • Back of the card: 3-day moving averages of views and engagement on X, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok where available, plus card supply by rarity.
03/07Rarer card, bigger multiplier

Rarity and boost

  • Common 1× · Rare 1.5× · Epic 2× · Legendary 2.5×.
  • The boost multiplies the creator’s Motion Score in your lineup.
  • Cards of the same creator share stars and Motion Score. Rarity sets the boost (cards can also come from different editions).
  • Rarity can beat fame: 500 pts on a Legendary card add 1,250, more than 1,000 pts on a Common.
  • Each tournament sets boost limits: your combination of rarities must fit them.
04/075 creators under the star cap

Build a lineup

  • Exactly 5 different creators. You can’t use the same creator twice, even if you own several of their cards.
  • Star cap: total stars must fit, e.g. 8+7+6+6+5 = 32 fits a 32★ cap.
  • Your lineup’s combination of card rarities must meet the tournament’s boost limits.
  • Submit your five in Motion’s Homebase: a draft isn’t an entry. You can edit and resubmit until Monday 7 AM ET.
  • One entry per tournament: resubmitting updates it.
05/07Lock Monday, results Friday

Tournament week

  • Sat–Sun: build and submit your lineup.
  • Mon 7 AM ET: lineups lock. 9 AM ET: the tournament starts.
  • Mon–Fri: scores update every 4 hours on the Tournament Leaderboard in Arena.
  • Fri 5 PM ET: results are finalized and your cards unlock for the next week.
  • Check each tournament’s Prize Table in Arena. Prizes for eligible winners usually arrive about 30 minutes after 5 PM ET, after wallet screening.
06/07Top creator = 1,000 pts

How points work

  • Motion Score: performance on X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube compared with every other creator. Fresh start every week: only posts published during the tournament count.
  • Big accounts can’t win on size alone. X carries the most weight.
  • On X only original posts, thread starters and quotes count. Replies, thread follow-ups, reposts and posts deleted within 3 hours don’t.
  • The top creator gets 1,000. Lineup Score = sum of each creator’s score × card boost.
07/07Where Motion Scout helps

Scout smarter

  • Leaderboard and Movers: Motion’s official creator ranks, a trend line for each, and who climbed or fell since Monday’s lock.
  • Build a deck: the best 5 for any star cap from 8 to 40. My lineup this week tracks the five you submitted.
  • My cards: add the rarity and stars of the cards you own, and it plans with your real boosts and stars.
  • Other stars are ≈ estimated from rank. It’s a planning tool, not the game’s score.
